Password Security Best Practices

Essential guidance for keeping your online accounts safe

Use Unique Passwords

Never reuse passwords across multiple sites. If one service suffers a data breach, hackers will attempt your credentials everywhere else.

Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Always enable Two-Factor (2FA) or MFA using an authenticator app (like Google Authenticator or YubiKey) instead of SMS whenever available.

Use a Password Manager

Instead of memorizing dozens of complex passwords, store them in an encrypted vault like Bitwarden, 1Password, or KeePass.

Prefer Passphrases Over Passwords

A phrase like correct-horse-battery-staple is easier to remember and significantly harder to brute-force than P@ss1.
